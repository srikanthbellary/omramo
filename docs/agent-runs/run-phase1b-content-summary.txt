## Story → landmark map

Every tier has both historical and modern or contemporary landmarks. At most two per story.

- **Natural fits (6):** Bremen Town Hall, Great Pyramid, Mount Fuji, Novgorod, Asante buildings, and Parthenon and Knossos (each with a short "built or linked later" note).
- **Everything else is framed** by one of four named devices: `thenAndNow`, `mapMoment`, `worldLink`, `presentDayNarrator`. A framed landmark appears only on its card, never in the scene art.
- **No landmark, by design:** the Cherokee, Dakota and Yup'ik stories. Attaching another nation's site would be lumping, and Mount Rushmore is excluded because it sits on land sacred to the Lakota and Dakota. The Efik and Punjab stories also have none, because no honest link exists.
